BODE OUTREACH OS

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 9, 2026

Bode Outreach OS helps users organize outreach leads, prepare personalized email content, manage templates and campaigns, and create Gmail drafts for human review. This policy explains what information the application handles and how it is used.

1. Information we handle

  • Account information: your Google sign-in identifier and email address, provided through the authentication service.
  • Workspace information: leads and contact details you import or enter, store URLs and observations, message subjects and bodies, templates, campaign information, and application settings.
  • Connected Gmail account information: the connected Gmail address and an OAuth refresh token used to maintain the connection. The refresh token is encrypted before it is stored on the server.
  • Technical information: limited operational data needed to authenticate requests, protect the service, and troubleshoot errors.

2. How Google data is used

If you connect Gmail, the application requests Google permissions to support Gmail functionality. In the current version, Gmail API access is used to create email drafts in the connected account for your review. The application does not automatically send outreach campaigns and does not use Gmail data for advertising, sell it, or transfer it to third parties for independent marketing purposes. The current draft workflow does not read your inbox message contents.

Google account information and data received through Google APIs are used only to provide and maintain the features you choose to use, improve their security, and respond to support requests. The application adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

3. Storage and security

Workspace information is stored in the application's database and associated with the signed-in user. Connected Gmail refresh tokens are encrypted on the server before storage. Access controls are used to separate users' workspace data. No online service can guarantee absolute security, so avoid importing information that is not necessary for your workflow.

4. Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information is shared with service providers only as needed to operate the application, including hosting, database/authentication, and Google services when you choose to connect them, or when disclosure is required by law or necessary to protect the service and its users.

5. Retention and your choices

Workspace information remains available while it is stored in your account. You can revoke the application's Google access from your Google Account security settings. To request deletion of stored workspace or connected-account data, contact the project maintainer through the Bode Outreach OS project page. Some limited records may be retained where required for security, legal compliance, or dispute resolution.

6. Children

The application is intended for people who can lawfully use an outreach management service and is not designed for children.

7. Changes and contact

This policy may be updated when the application or its data practices change. The latest version will be published on this page. For privacy questions or data requests, use the Bode Outreach OS project page.
